## New Concert Set to Rock Lake Grapevine for Charity October 11

GRAPEVINE, TEXAS (August 27, 2026) – Live music and community giving will come together this Fall at The Concert on Lake Grapevine, an outdoor concert planned for Sunday, October 11 at Mariner Park Point, by the dam on Lake Grapevine.

Presented by Tolbert's Restaurant & Chili Parlor and organized by nonprofit The Charity Grapevine Incorporated, the inaugural event will feature four popular tribute bands from the Dallas-Fort Worth area: Texas Moonflower celebrating the music of Santana; Little Queen honoring Heart; Petty Theft performing the music of Tom Petty and The Dead Thing bringing the music of The Grateful Dead to the stage.

## Event Details

- **When:** Sunday, October 11 | 12 – 9 p.m.
- **Where:** Mariner Park Point, 1 Sunfish Drive, Grapevine
- **Tickets:** $35
- **Food & Drinks:** BYOB, plus food trucks and vendors on site

## Supporting Our Community

Proceeds benefit two North Texas organizations: Grace Food Bank of Grapevine and The Miracle League of Southlake, a baseball league providing opportunities for children and adults with disabilities to participate in the sport.

## What the Owners Say

"Tolbert's Restaurant & Chili Parlor is known to have live music every day of the week. And we are thrilled to bring that same energy to Lake Grapevine for a wonderful cause," said Tolbert's owner Kathleen Tolbert Ryan.

"We wanted to give back to the community and our strong connections with the local music scene made this a natural fit," added owner Paul Ryan.

The concert is the first event organized by The Charity Grapevine Incorporated, a newly formed 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Organizers hope to make the concert an annual opportunity to bring the community together through live music while supporting local causes.
